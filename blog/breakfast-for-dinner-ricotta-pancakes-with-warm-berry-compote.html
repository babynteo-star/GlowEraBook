Breakfast for Dinner Ricotta Pancakes with Warm Berry Compote

In This Article

  1. Why choose breakfast for supper
  2. Fluffy ricotta pancakes with warm berry compote
  3. How to make ricotta pancakes for breakfast for dinner
  4. Tips for the best breakfast for supper experience

There's quiet luxury in flipping the day on its head. When evening arrives, nothing feels more restorative than turning classic morning flavors into a refined supper. The gentle scent of butter in the pan, the soft sweetness of warm berries, and the permission to linger over a plate that belongs more to brunch than night, these small pleasures make breakfast for dinner feel like the most elegant form of self-care.

This easy yet elevated ricotta pancakes recipe delivers light, cloud-like pancakes paired with a glossy berry compote. It's ideal for cozy weeknights, relaxed weekends, or anyone searching for stylish breakfast foods for dinner that still feel special.

Why choose breakfast for supper

Serving breakfast foods at night is more than a trend, it's a simple way to bring warmth and ease to the evening. Whether you call it breakfast for dinner, brinner, or breakfast for supper, the approach offers comfort without heaviness and elegance without effort. These ricotta pancakes are naturally lighter than traditional versions, making them well suited to an evening meal.

Fluffy ricotta pancakes with warm berry compote

Serves 2 to 3. Prep time: 10 minutes. Cook time: 15 minutes.

Ingredients for the berry compote

  • 300g mixed berries, fresh or frozen, blueberries, raspberries, and blackberries work well
  • 2 tbsp pure maple syrup
  • 1 tsp fresh lemon juice
  • Optional: a small strip of lemon zest

Ingredients for the ricotta pancakes

  • 200g fresh ricotta
  • 2 large eggs, separated
  • 120ml whole milk
  • 1 tbsp pure maple syrup, or honey
  • 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • Pinch of fine sea salt
  • Unsalted butter, for cooking
  • Extra maple syrup and fresh berries, to serve

How to make ricotta pancakes for breakfast for dinner

1. Prepare the berry compote

Place the berries, maple syrup, lemon juice, and zest (if using) in a small saucepan over medium-low heat. Stir gently for 6 to 8 minutes until the berries release their juices and the mixture becomes glossy and slightly thickened. Remove from heat and set aside. The warm berry compote adds bright, jewel-toned flavor that elevates any breakfast for supper plate.

2. Make the pancake batter

In a large bowl, whisk the ricotta, egg yolks, milk, maple syrup, and vanilla until smooth. In a separate bowl, combine the flour, baking powder, and salt. Fold the dry ingredients into the ricotta mixture until just combined, avoid overmixing.

In a clean bowl, whisk the egg whites to soft peaks. Gently fold them into the batter in two additions. The result should feel airy and light, the key to fluffy ricotta pancakes.

3. Cook the pancakes

Warm a non-stick or well-seasoned skillet over medium-low heat and add a little butter. When it foams, spoon small portions of batter, about 3 to 4 tablespoons each, into the pan. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until golden underneath and dotted with bubbles. Flip carefully and cook another 1 to 2 minutes. Transfer to a warm plate and repeat with the remaining batter.

4. Serve

Stack the pancakes, spoon over the warm berry compote, and finish with a light drizzle of maple syrup and a few fresh berries. Enjoy slowly, this is breakfast for dinner at its most gracious.

Tips for the best breakfast for supper experience

  • Use full-fat ricotta for the creamiest texture.
  • Keep the heat moderate so the pancakes cook through without browning too quickly.
  • Make the compote ahead and reheat gently for an even easier evening meal.
  • For variation, add a pinch of lemon zest to the batter or serve with a dollop of crème fraîche.

This ricotta pancakes recipe turns an ordinary night into something quietly memorable. Whether you're searching for easy breakfast for dinner ideas, elegant breakfast for supper recipes, or simply a lighter evening meal, these fluffy pancakes deliver comfort, style, and satisfaction in every bite.
