Healing & Boundaries

Healing rarely looks like the breakthrough moments they show in movies. More often, it looks like a boundary held for the first time, a relationship you finally stopped over-explaining yourself in, or the quiet decision to stop absorbing everyone else's disappointment as your own. This category holds both sides of that work: the inner healing, from childhood wounds, burnout, anxiety, and the seasons that left a mark, and the outer practice of boundaries that protect what you're healing. You'll find honest guidance on setting limits without guilt, recognizing when you're overextending yourself emotionally, responding when a boundary isn't respected, and letting go of relationships that no longer feel safe or mutual. None of it promises boundaries will ever feel completely comfortable. They usually don't, not at first. What this category offers instead is a steadier way through: language for the hard conversations, permission to protect your peace, and the reminder that healing and boundary-setting are not separate projects, they're the same one, seen from two different angles.
