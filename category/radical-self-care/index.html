Radical Self-Care

Radical self-care isn't a bubble bath and a candle, though those have their place. It's the harder, quieter work of treating your own rest, energy, and time as non-negotiable, even when the people around you are used to you having none of it. This is the space for the self-care that doesn't photograph well: the nap you took instead of powering through, the invitation you declined, the ritual you kept on the one night you had every reason to skip it. Here you'll find honest guidance on building a self-care practice that survives real life, sustainable rituals for sleep, nourishment, and movement, and permission to rest without earning it first. Nothing here asks you to overhaul your life overnight. It's built on the belief that small, repeatable acts of care, chosen on purpose and kept even when it's inconvenient, are what actually change how a life feels from the inside. If you've ever felt guilty for resting, or noticed your self-care habits are always the first thing to go when things get busy, start here.
